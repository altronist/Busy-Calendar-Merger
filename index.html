Local PBKDF2 Auth & GDPR Privacy

Account Access & Privacy Consent

All account records, hashed credentials, and calendar data stay local in your browser storage (localStorage) unless you explicitly export or configure a feed URL.

GDPR Privacy Notice & Lawful Basis (Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 13)

Controller & Storage: Processed locally on your device in browser localStorage. Passwords are salted and hashed with Web Crypto PBKDF2-SHA256 (100,000 iterations)—plaintext passwords are never stored.

Purpose & Retention: Calendar busy/free time windows, slot labels, feed URLs, and preferences are stored solely to merge busy blocks and export .ics files until you erase your data or delete your account.

Prefer not to create an account? You can use local guest mode without saving account credentials.

GDPR Privacy Notice & Data Controls

Exercise your right to information, portability/export, retention control, and erasure.

Privacy & Lawful Basis Summary

Lawful Basis: Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for account & calendar workspace persistence, and legitimate interest / user-initiated action for generating consolidated busy-only .ics blocks.

Data Categories: Account email/display name, salted PBKDF2-SHA256 credential verifier, calendar slot names, optional feed URLs, imported ICS/JSON schedules, and merged busy intervals. No third-party tracking cookies or analytics scripts are embedded.

Your Rights: Access & export your stored data anytime (Art. 15 & Art. 20), withdraw consent or configure retention/session rules, and permanently erase all data (Art. 17).
